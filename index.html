How to war for noobs

disclaimer: this is not a full on strategy providing guide, its just so u can orient better and not be a Bob(no offense to Bobs)

Rules

I will write this like it is in my head without any formatting, cuz I am lazy and I have never done this before. Let's make some rules, that make sense to me and might not make sense to you from what I saw in our first war. For each one I will provide MY reasoning. If they dont make sense to you and you have better argument, feel free to our leadership, and work it out with them. There might be some priority in these rules, but idk.

RULE 1:
Don't buy healing potions from armory, you should bring potions from outside!!! Might be an exception for ranged, who might wanna buy haste elixir. I always bring around 40, that's why I don't die, used 21 last war. They dont count into healing, but we are not here for stat padding. There is no better heal than potion in the game right now. Play around them(30 sec cooldown).

RULE 2:
Siege cannons do around 1,5k AOE dmg, you should be able to dodge it!!, idk about machine guns, but those cannons can reaload approx every 4 sec, and have around 15 round magazines. If you are fighting front to back or in circle around point, u are gonna get hit(if enemy aint drunk ofc). Be prepared for that and dont go in the circle unless u can take some shots, or the area is free.

RULE 3:
Melee, you are the ones, who need to destroy enemy siege, which is placed on the ground(not really high priority cuz of rule 4, but can be usefull given some specific circumstances). For this use powder kegs, they do 80k dmg, dont damage you and damage enemy players(yea for 80k, so instakill). The only problem is that you have to ignite them, which takes approx 4-5sec, so be prepared.

RULE 4:
Ranged. PLS for the love of God, kill those players, who are sitting in those little chairs on siege weapons, you dont even need to be Shroud to hit those shots, they are not moving. Let them know that we know about them.

RULE 5:
DON'T DIE, especially in late game and especially during attacking. Staying alive means, you are pressuring enemy into bad situations. Try to run away or block or heal. Running away means enemy has to either chase, which makes them very vurneable or stay. IF you die, they can leave, and help elsewhere. Pressure is more important than doing dmg. Holding one gate near choke point is more important then going in and dying in 10 seconds. REMEMBER, once u are in fort, their respawn is closer, just hold.

RULE 6:
Destroying one gate doesnt mean we should go in xD. Even two gates are tricky. You have to respect rule 5, just hold, make them interested in you, but don't die.

RULE 7:
In every pvp game, people like to have HP, you should like it too, I don't care what u do in pve(unless u in my dungeon). Also this helps to achieve rule 5 a lot easier :). And gives u huge value on potions.

RULE 8:
AOE CC > AOE DMG >> AOE HEALS >>>>>>>>> SINGLE TARGET HEALS >>>> SINGLE TARGET DMG >>>>>>>>>>>>>>>> hatchet :).

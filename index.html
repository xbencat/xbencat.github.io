Rules

I will write this like it is in my head without any formatting, cuz I am lazy and I have never done this before. RULE 1:
Don't buy healing potions from armory, you should bring potions from outside!!! Might be an exception for ranged, who might wanna buy haste elixir. I always bring around 40, that's why I don't die, used 21 last war. They dont count into healing, but we are not here for stat padding. There is no better heal than potion in the game right now. Play around them(30 sec cooldown). Pop one when u are around 70% hp, dont waint for last opportunity, or pop one while u waiting for your abillities, left clicking usually doesnt do much. Better stay alive than do 500 more dmg.

RULE 2:
Siege cannons do around 1,5k AOE dmg, you should be able to dodge it!!, idk about machine guns, but those cannons can reaload approx every 4 sec, and have around 15 round magazines. If you are fighting front to back or in circle around point, u are gonna get hit(if enemy aint drunk ofc). Be prepared for that and dont go in the circle unless u can take some shots, or the area is free.

RULE 3:
Melee, you are the ones, who need to destroy enemy siege, which is placed on the ground(not really high priority cuz of rule 4, but can be usefull given some specific circumstances). For this use powder kegs, they do 80k dmg, dont damage you and damage enemy players(yea for 80k, so instakill). The only problem is that you have to ignite them, which takes approx 4-5sec, so be prepared.

RULE 4:
Ranged. PLS for the love of God, kill those players, who are sitting in those little chairs on siege weapons, you dont even need to be Shroud to hit those shots, they are not moving. Let them know that we know about them.

RULE 5:
DON'T DIE, especially in late game and especially during attacking. Staying alive means, you are pressuring enemy into bad situations. Try to run away or block or heal. Running away means enemy has to either chase, which makes them very vurneable or stay. IF you die, they can leave, and help elsewhere. Pressure is more important than doing dmg. Holding one gate near choke point is more important then going in and dying in 10 seconds. REMEMBER, once u are in fort, their respawn is closer, just hold. Chance to move in will come I am sure of that.

RULE 6:
Destroying one gate doesnt mean we should go in xD. Even two gates are tricky. You have to respect rule 5, just hold, make them interested in you, but don't die.

RULE 7:

Attacking

During attack my advice for simple Bob is simple.
If you are ranged, first focus siege shooters, then you focus enemy frontline.
Melee, it depends, if you can take some dps (heavy armor, maybe medium), try to take pressure from their ranged, and try to fight on circle, or near choke point(gate).
If you are mobile melee, try to destoy their ground siege. If you are mobile melee with cc disturb backline !! Just like my team yesterday. Nothing is more important than disturbing backline imo.
Usually it is good to take B first since its in the middle and makes their rotations harder. Also you can acces armory when u standing in the middle of circle.
Opening more gates is better, when inside, mobile melee with cc should try to DISTURB their ranged, who are usually standing on those wooden platforms, also very good idea to give them a little something right when they spawn, cuz they wont expect it, and then u can attack frontline from behind.
If you are on siege weapon you should focus their siege, then gate or put som dps in their heavy melee to help with pressure.
If you are lost or we have good position on our siege help your siege shoooter with some ammo from armory, each cannon has only 15 in magazine.
Don't bother placing siege when we already breached the gates, there used to be invinsible wall, so you won't be able to hit people inside fort outside their gates(idk if it changed.)

Defending

Roles are probably same, make sure u fight on circle, cuz when we lose it, they can respawn there.
Don't forget to repair gates, even if they take some down.
Bring ammo to people who are shooting from our walls, if you are not having impact elsewhere.
Try to stop ratations with some cc.
The last circle should be the easiest to defend since we can just plow them with siege, but dont rely on it.
